Shattered Dreams


Don't remember how,
Why,
Or when.
One day life was just broken,
Shattered,
And destroyed.


Don't remember why I became this way,
Who made me this way,
And how I became this way.


All I see sometimes is a long road,
A long pathway,
A steep climb.


And instead of figuring things out,
Finding things out,
Knowing things out.


Instead I ponder on my shattered mirrors I call life,
I call destruction,
I call death.


Because that's "life",
That's "death"
And it's forevermore.
